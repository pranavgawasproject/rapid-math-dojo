Technique Dojo: Mental Math Mastery

The 4 core techniques mental athletes use

1. Number Bonds (10 & 100 Anchors)

Never calculate 7 + 3 from scratch. Turn them into instantaneous subconscious pairs:

1 + 9
2 + 8
3 + 7
4 + 6
5 + 5
2. Bridging Tens (Break & Complete)

When adding across a ten threshold (e.g., 8 + 7), do not count up by ones. Break the second number into what completes 10:

8 needs 2 to make 10.
Split 7 into (2 + 5).
Then: (8 + 2) + 5 = 10 + 5 = 15.
3. Left-to-Right (Tens Before Ones)

Standard paper math goes right-to-left with carries. Mental math does the opposite: Add largest values first!

Problem: 58 + 27
1. Add Tens: 50 + 20 = 70
2. Add Ones: 8 + 7 = 15
3. Combine: 70 + 15 = 85
4. Rounding & Compensation

For numbers ending in 8 or 9 (e.g., 29, 48), round up to the nearest ten and subtract the overshoot:

Problem: 46 + 29
Treat 29 as (30 - 1)
46 + 30 = 76, then 76 - 1 = 75!
